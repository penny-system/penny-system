WHAT YOU'LL RECEIVE EACH DAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MORNING BRIEF (8:00 AM)
   An MP3 audio summary is sent first, covering your open positions, today's
   buy candidates, and watchlist highlights. This is followed by a text brief
   and, if there are actionable candidates, a formatted BUY message.

2. HOURLY SCAN
   Runs throughout the day. A notification is only sent if new actionable BUY
   candidates appear that haven't already been actioned.


READING A BUY CANDIDATE MESSAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   Example message:

   🎆 2 BUY Candidates -- Sat, Feb 22
   💰 Purse: $5,000  |  Max: 6 positions

   ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   📈 GRCE  ⚠️
   Score 84.5  ·  Conf 72%  ·  Risk 0.30
   Ref $0.42  ·  Stop $0.37  ·  Take $0.55
   Qty: 500 shares
   🛡 Gate: HOLD (78%) -- Near-term dilution risk
   Why: Momentum breakout on volume confirmation

   ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   📈 BRLS
   Score 82.1  ·  Conf 68%  ·  Risk 0.20
   Ref $1.15  ·  Stop $1.00  ·  Take $1.45
   Qty: 250 shares

   ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   python approve.py


   Field Descriptions:

   Score    Composite signal score. 80 or above = BUY threshold.

   Conf     Scoring engine's confidence in the BUY signal.
            Higher = stronger setup.

   Risk     Risk score. Lower is better. Penalises volatility,
            dilution signals, news risk, etc.

   Ref      Reference entry price at time of scan.

   Stop     Stop-loss price. Order exits here if the trade goes
            against you.

   Take     Take-profit price. Target exit.

   Qty      Suggested share quantity based on your purse and
            position sizing rules.

   ⚠️       News risk detected for this ticker (see Trigger A below).

   Why      First sentence of the scoring engine's rationale.
            See "Scoring Signal Glossary" below for plain-English definitions.


SCORING SIGNAL GLOSSARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   These terms appear in the "Why" line of each candidate. They describe the
   signals the scanner used to identify the stock.

   Short-term move (+X%)
      The stock's price went up X% in the last few minutes of trading.
      The bigger the number, the stronger the intraday momentum.

   5-day move (+X%)
      The stock has been climbing steadily over the past week. A positive
      number means the trend has been upward for several days, not just today.

   Volume surge (Xx normal)
      More than twice the usual number of shares have traded hands today.
      Think of it as a crowd forming -- when a lot of people suddenly want
      to buy, it tends to push the price higher and makes the move more
      credible. The scanner requires at least 1.2x average volume before
      treating a setup as confirmed.

   Above-average volume (Xx)
      Trading activity is higher than usual, but not dramatically so.
      A mild positive signal.

   Price breakout confirmed
      The stock pushed through a price level it had been stuck below, and
      the volume backed it up. This is the strongest setup the scanner looks
      for -- both the price move and the crowd activity line up. A score of
      80+ (BUY) requires this condition to be met.

   Price breakout unconfirmed
      The stock pushed through a resistance level, but not many people were
      trading it. Without volume behind it, the move may not hold. The
      scanner deliberately caps the score here and will not trigger a BUY
      on this signal alone.

   High liquidity / Strong liquidity
      A lot of money is flowing through this stock today. Higher liquidity
      means you can buy and sell without the price jumping around on you.

   $XM daily volume
      The total dollar value of shares traded today (e.g. "$4M daily volume"
      means $4 million worth of shares changed hands). A useful proxy for
      how easy it will be to get in and out of the position cleanly.

   Scan criteria met
      No single signal stood out, but the stock passed the minimum filters
      across the board.


SOFT GATE DECISIONS (🛡 Gate)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   When a BUY candidate also has active news risk (Trigger A), Claude reviews
   it and returns one of four decisions:

   APPROVE  Risk flagged but assessed as acceptable -- proceed.
            /approve SYMBOL will place the order normally.

   HOLD     Pause -- the news situation needs to clarify before acting.
            Review the reason carefully before using /approve.

   REDUCE   Proceed but with a smaller position.
            Consider using /approve SYMBOL with a lower qty override.

   BLOCK    Hard veto -- do not place this order.
            Treat this as a strong signal to skip the candidate.

   The percentage shown (e.g. 78%) is Claude's self-assessed certainty in its
   own decision. Think of it as signal strength, not a trade probability.


TRIGGER A ALERTS (🔴)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   Trigger A fires when a single news headline contains 2 or more risk keywords
   simultaneously (e.g. "offering" + "warrant" in the same sentence). This is a
   stronger signal than a single keyword hit and always triggers a Soft Gate
   review if the ticker is also a BUY candidate.

   Risk keywords include: offering, warrant, dilution, reverse split, bankruptcy,
   going concern, halt, investigation, lawsuit, and others.


TELEGRAM COMMANDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   All commands are sent directly in your Telegram chat with the bot.
   No computer access required -- only TWS on your PC must be running
   when placing orders or checking positions.

   --- VIEWING ---

   /status
      Show a summary of the latest scan: run number, time, how many
      BUY candidates and watchlist entries were found.

   /candidates
      Re-display the current BUY candidates from the latest scan,
      including score, prices, and suggested qty for each.

   /positions
      Fetch your live open positions from IBKR and display symbol,
      share count, and average cost.
      Requires: TWS running with API enabled.

   /brief
      Re-send today's morning brief text in case you missed it.

   --- PLACING ORDERS ---

   /approve SYMBOL
      Stage a bracket order for the named symbol using the suggested
      qty from the scan.
      Example: /approve GRCE

   /approve SYMBOL QTY
      Stage a bracket order with a custom share quantity.
      Example: /approve GRCE 300

   /confirm
      Place the staged bracket order via IBKR. You will be shown a
      full summary of the order (entry, stop, take, qty) before
      this command is sent -- review carefully.
      Requires: TWS running with API enabled.

   /cancel
      Discard the pending order without placing it. Safe to use at
      any point before /confirm.

   /skip SYMBOL
      Dismiss a candidate for the current run. No order is placed
      and no further alerts will be sent for this ticker today.
      Example: /skip BRLS

   --- SETTINGS ---

   /showsettings
      Display current runtime values for purse, position limits, and
      FX rate.

   /purse AMOUNT
      Propose a new total capital allocation (in CAD).
      Example: /purse 5000

   /maxpos N
      Propose a new maximum number of concurrent positions.
      Example: /maxpos 6

   /fx RATE
      Propose a new USD/CAD exchange rate.
      Example: /fx 0.74

   /minpos AMOUNT
      Propose a new minimum position size in USD.
      Example: /minpos 150

   /confirm
      Also used to apply a proposed settings change (same command,
      works for both orders and settings -- the bot handles both).

   /cancel
      Also used to discard a proposed settings change.

   /resetsettings
      Remove all runtime overrides and return to the defaults
      defined in config.py.


TYPICAL DAILY WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   1. Receive the morning audio brief (MP3) at 8:00 AM.
   2. Read the BUY candidate message that follows.
   3. For each candidate you want to act on:
        a. Type /approve GRCE  (or with a custom qty: /approve GRCE 300)
        b. Review the order summary the bot sends back.
        c. Type /confirm to place the bracket order.
           OR type /cancel to discard.
   4. For candidates you want to ignore: /skip GRCE
   5. At any time:
        /status    -- check latest scan
        /positions -- see what you currently hold
        /brief     -- re-read the morning summary


BRACKET ORDER STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   Every order placed via /approve + /confirm is a bracket order:

   Parent:  BUY Limit at Ref price
   Child 1: SELL Limit at Take price  (take-profit)
   Child 2: SELL Stop  at Stop price  (stop-loss)

   All three legs are linked. If the take-profit fills, the stop-loss
   is automatically cancelled, and vice versa.

   No orders are placed automatically. Every trade requires your
   explicit /confirm.


PIPELINE FAILURE ALERTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   If the IBKR connection or pipeline fails, you will receive:

      ⚠️ IBKR/pipeline failed: ConnectionRefusedError
      (Check TWS running + API enabled on port 7497)

   Common causes:
   - TWS / IB Gateway is not running.
   - API is not enabled in TWS settings:
     Edit > Global Configuration > API > Settings
   - Port mismatch: paper trading uses 7497, live trading uses 7496.

   After fixing the issue, the next scheduled run will retry automatically.
   Repeated failures within a 24-hour window are silenced to avoid spam.


KEY SETTINGS (config.py / .env)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   PURSE                  Total capital allocated for this strategy.
   MAX_POSITIONS          Maximum concurrent open positions.
   MIN_SCORE              Minimum score to trigger a BUY notification (default: 80).
   IB_PORT                TWS API port (7497 = paper, 7496 = live).
   MARKETAUX_API_KEY      News API key for headline analysis.
   ANTHROPIC_API_KEY      Claude API key for Soft Gate reviews.
   TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN     Telegram bot credentials.
   TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID       Your Telegram numeric chat ID.
