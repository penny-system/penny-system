SECTION 4 — SCORING LOGIC
------------------------------------------------------------

score_total:
Measures attractiveness based on momentum, setup type, and opportunity strength.

risk_score:
Measures structural danger:
- Liquidity
- Volume spikes
- Extreme moves
- Regime context
- Correlation to holdings

BUY threshold:
score_total ≥ 80

risk_score does NOT auto-block BUY.
It informs Trigger C and future churn logic.

------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5 — TRIGGERS
------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger A:
Headline contains 2+ risk keywords in same article.

Trigger C:
BUY candidate + Trigger A → OpenAI Soft Gate review.

OpenAI returns:
- APPROVE
- HOLD
- REDUCE
- BLOCK
- confidence
- reasoning

AI is advisory only.
